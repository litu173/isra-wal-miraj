الإسراء والمعراج Al-Isra’ wal-Mi‘raj

    Sources

    Every scene is drawn from the Qur’an and from narrations in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, with two further narrations graded sahih (Abu Dawud, at-Tirmidhi). The English is a careful paraphrase; please return to the full texts and to scholars for study.

    The visuals are symbolic light and geometry. No prophet, angel or person is depicted. Hadith numbers follow sunnah.com.
